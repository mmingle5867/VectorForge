# Artwork Completion Usability v0.68.0

## Completion behavior

Saving a vector result returns to the Dashboard. Final SVG, PNG, and JPG output names use the artwork title—the established human-facing name before working-copy edits—not a versioned working JPG filename.

## Preview / Tune cleanup

- The duplicate lower **Open Saved PNG in Raster Editor** and **Open Saved JPG in Raster Editor** buttons are removed.
- The existing working-image actions remain in the preparation area.
- Every preparation and vectorizer control now has explanatory hover help describing its effect and tradeoff rather than repeating its label.
